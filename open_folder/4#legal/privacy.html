🇬🇧 Privacy Policy

Our hosting provider collects the following data transmitted by your browser in so-called log files:

IP address, the address of the previously visited website (Referer request header), date and time of the request, time zone difference to Greenwich Mean Time, content of the request, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, website from which the request originates, and information about the browser and operating system.

This is necessary to display our website and to ensure its stability and security. This corresponds to our legitimate interest within the meaning of Art. 6(1)(1)(f) GDPR.

No tracking takes place, and we have no direct access to this data.

The data is deleted as soon as the purpose of the processing no longer applies.

The processing of the data specified in this section is neither legally nor contractually required. Without this processing, the functionality of the website cannot be guaranteed.

GitHub has implemented compliance measures for international data transfers. These apply to all worldwide activities in which GitHub processes personal data of natural persons in the EU.
