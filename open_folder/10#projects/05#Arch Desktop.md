My Arch Desktop configurations using:
- the `Hyprland` desktop manager for satisfying tiling and smooth curves
- the `Kitty` terminal emulator for images and speed in the terminal
- the `Fish` shell for smart suggestions and vim keybinding
- the `Rofi` menu daemon for System, Wallpaper, Clipboard, Emoji and Icon Menus
- `Python` scripts and `matugen` for wallpaper and clipboard image processing
- `Waybar` in a cool powerline styling, and with custom modules for `fcitx5` and `tailscale`

All of this is of **inspired** by other peoples linux ricing but fully **custom**.
